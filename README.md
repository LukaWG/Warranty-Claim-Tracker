# Warranty Claim Tracker

[![Deploy to Docker](https://github.com/LukaWG/Warranty-Claim-Tracker/actions/workflows/deploy.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/LukaWG/Warranty-Claim-Tracker/actions/workflows/deploy.yml)

A web application for managing warranty repair claims across multiple brands and sites. It gives your team a single place to submit claims, track their status, approve or reject them, communicate about them, and report on performance.

---

## What it does

**Dashboard** — An at-a-glance view of all claims. See counts by status (pending, approved, rejected), filter by brand or date, and jump straight to any individual claim.

**Submit a claim** — Fill in the claim form with repair details, parts, labour costs, and supporting information. Claims are immediately visible to approvers.

**Approvals** — Approvers see a queue of pending claims. They can approve or reject each one with a note, and every decision is recorded with a timestamp and the approver's name.

**Messages** — Internal messaging tied to claims, so conversations about a repair stay in one place rather than in email threads.

**Reporting** — Charts and tables showing claim volumes, approval rates, average lag times, and cost breakdowns. Reports can be exported to PDF.

**Configuration** — Admins can set up brands, sites, and user roles through the settings area.
